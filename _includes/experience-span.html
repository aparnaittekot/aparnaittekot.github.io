{%- comment -%} Works out a date range for the Experience tab. Sets, in the caller's scope: xp_s / xp_e (month numbers: year * 12 + month - 1), xp_dates ("Oct 2023 – Present") and xp_duration ("3 yrs 1 mo", counting both end months like LinkedIn does). Usage: include experience-span.html start="2023-10" end=role.end now=xp_now (no end = present) {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign _start = include.start | append: '' -%} {%- assign _y = _start | slice: 0, 4 | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign _m = _start | slice: 5, 2 | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign xp_s = _y | times: 12 | plus: _m | minus: 1 -%} {%- assign _from = _start | append: '-01' | date: '%b %Y' -%} {%- if include.end -%} {%- assign _end = include.end | append: '' -%} {%- assign _y = _end | slice: 0, 4 | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign _m = _end | slice: 5, 2 | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign xp_e = _y | times: 12 | plus: _m | minus: 1 -%} {%- assign _to = _end | append: '-01' | date: '%b %Y' -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign xp_e = include.now -%} {%- assign _to = 'Present' -%} {%- endif -%} {%- assign xp_dates = _from | append: ' – ' | append: _to -%} {%- assign _months = xp_e | minus: xp_s | plus: 1 -%} {%- assign _years = _months | divided_by: 12 -%} {%- assign _rest = _months | modulo: 12 -%} {%- assign xp_duration = '' -%} {%- if _years > 0 -%} {%- assign xp_duration = _years | append: ' yr' -%} {%- if _years > 1 -%}{%- assign xp_duration = xp_duration | append: 's' -%}{%- endif -%} {%- if _rest > 0 -%}{%- assign xp_duration = xp_duration | append: ' ' -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endif -%} {%- if _rest > 0 -%}{%- assign xp_duration = xp_duration | append: _rest | append: ' mo' -%}{%- endif -%}
